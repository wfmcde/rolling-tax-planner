# Module 1: Navigation & Decision Tree Specification

## 1. Core Filing & Household Profile (Form 1040)
- **Filing Status:** Married Filing Jointly, Single, Head of Household, or Married Filing Separately.
- **Household & Age Flags:** Taxpayer and spouse birth years/ages for standard deduction adjustments, retirement rules, and Medicare/IRMAA thresholds.
- **Income Streams:** W-2 wages, interest, dividends, capital gains/losses, Social Security, taxable pensions/IRAs, and Schedule K-1 pass-through amounts.
- **Deductions & Healthcare:** Standard vs. itemized deductions and medical/health insurance premium inputs.

## 2. Business & Entity Ownership Branch
- **Entity Type:** None, Sole Proprietorship / Single-Member LLC (Schedule C), **LLC with S-Corporation Designation** (Form 1120-S / Schedule K-1), Partnership / Multi-Member LLC (Form 1065), or C-Corporation (Form 1120).
- **S-Corp Sub-Menu (When LLC with S-Corp Designation Is Selected):**
  - **Compensation & Profit Split:** Officer W-2 wages vs. shareholder distributions and payroll tax awareness.
  - **Defined Benefit Plan (DBP):** Active plan status, contribution capacity, funding/rollover milestones, loan/collateral rules, and termination/wind-down timeline.
  - **Qualified Plans & Annuities:** 401(k)/Profit Sharing, IRA rollovers, and annuity contracts.
  - **Medical Premiums:** Employer-paid or self-employed health insurance handling.
  - **Pass-Through Entity (PTE) Elections:** State-level PTE elections and entity-level tax payments.

## 3. State Footprint & Residency Branch
- **Active Business / Sourcing States:** States where revenue is sourced or entity filings apply (such as Arizona and California).
- **Residency & Part-Year Moves:** Full-year vs. part-year residency transitions (such as moving between states with different income tax rules).

## 4. Six-Year Rolling Forecast & IRS Form Output
- **Horizon View:** Year 1 (Actuals) followed by Years 2–6 (Rolling Projections), rolling forward annually.
- **Form & Filing Map:** Maps selections to required federal returns/schedules (Form 1040, Schedules 1–3, Form 1120-S, Schedule K-1), state returns/PTE elections, and retirement plan reporting checkpoints.
